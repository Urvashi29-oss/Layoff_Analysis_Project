# Layoff_Analysis_Project (Python(Jupyter Notebook) and Power BI)
This project is about analyzing the layoff dataset of tech firms aroundthe globe are fighting the economic slowdown. The slow consumer spending, higher interest rates by central banks and strong dollars overseas are hinting towards possible recession and tech firms have started laying employees off. 

#What's in the dataset
4,554 layoff events spanning March 2020 to August 2026, covering 67 countries and 31 industries — a running log of company-level layoffs (company, location, headcount cut, date, industry, funding stage, and source article).

Key patterns:
- 2023 was the peak year — 10.5M reported layoffs across 1,390 events, followed by a still-heavy 2024 (8.9M across 633 events). Volume has been tapering through 2025–26.
- The US dominates, accounting for the large majority of events and roughly 20M of the 32.7M total people laid off, followed by India and Israel.
- Hardware tops the industry list by people affected, ahead of Finance, Consumer, and Retail — though Finance and Retail have far more individual events (540 and 362) with smaller average cuts, while Hardware's total is inflated by a few outsized numbers.
- One data-quality note: three separate small companies (BionicHIVE, Brilliant, Tomorrow) each show an identical, implausible 1,928,571 laid off at "100%" — almost certainly a bad calculation in the source rather than real headcount. I'd treat the Hardware/Israel totals with that caveat in mind.
